Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026.

Emeralda Creator Tools is a personal desktop application used by the operator of Emeralda (@emma_smart_123) to prepare short videos, view permitted account information, and send authorized creator-owned videos to TikTok. The app is not operated by TikTok.

Information we process

Only after the account holder approves the official TikTok authorization flow, the app may access the account's TikTok profile ID, display name, avatar, video list and video metadata, available video engagement statistics, OAuth scope and access tokens. The scope depends on TikTok approvals and the permissions chosen by the account holder. We do not ask for or store a TikTok password.

Purpose and use

The data is used only to identify the authorized TikTok account; show permitted videos and statistics; help the creator choose and prepare clips; and, only when specifically requested, upload selected creator-owned media to TikTok's inbox for further review. The app does not automatically publish videos publicly or sell TikTok account information.

Storage and security

The app stores tokens and settings locally on the account holder's Mac in restricted-permission files. Original and prepared videos are stored locally unless deliberately uploaded. This public policy website is separate from the private Evgenii Agent dashboard, which it does not expose. TikTok API communications use encrypted HTTPS.

Disclosure

Authorized account data is transmitted to TikTok as required to complete requested actions via its official API. The app does not sell account or follower information to advertisers or data brokers. Content processed by TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Retention, revocation and deletion

The account holder can revoke access in TikTok account settings. Local API tokens and locally stored data can be deleted by the account holder. To request assistance with removal of locally stored account information, contact the operator using the account link below. Data already held by TikTok is governed by TikTok's policies.

Contact and policy updates

Operator: the owner of Emeralda (@emma_smart_123). Contact through the TikTok profile where messages are available. Material changes to this policy will be published at this address.
